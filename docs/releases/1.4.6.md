## Changes

- Redesigned the Android live viewer with project theme colors, accessible icon controls, and light/dark theme support.
- Show landscape, portrait, rotated, and ultrawide live video in full without stretching or cropping.
- Added immersive fullscreen viewing, source-aware orientation, and controls that hide automatically and reappear on tap.
- Swipe vertically on the left of the video to adjust viewing brightness, or on the right to adjust voice playback volume, with percentage feedback.
- Improved rotation, background recovery, and ended/disconnected stream handling while preserving the existing LiveKit connection and audio controls.
- Included voice session recovery after public IP changes and a reconnect delay fix.
